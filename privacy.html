Noor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Noor ("the app") is a free Qur'an, Sunnah and prayer-times app made by Munazzar. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Noor does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Noor has no sign-in, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking code, and runs no servers or databases of its own.

Data kept on your device

Everything you create stays on your phone: your name, reminders, saved items, prayer and fasting logs, reading and listening progress, trackers, streak, Dock backgrounds you choose, and settings. It is removed when you uninstall the app. If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may keep a copy of the app's settings in your Google account; Noor never sees or receives it.

Location

If you allow it, Noor uses your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on the device. Your location is never sent anywhere. You can instead pick a city by hand.

Photos and videos

If you choose a photo or video as a Dock background, it is copied into the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded.

Internet use

Noor goes online only to download content you ask for: Qur'an recitations (everyayah.com, mp3quran.net), tafsir (api.quran.com), optional daily cards (api.alquran.cloud) and hadith collections (the open hadith-api on jsDelivr / GitHub), and adhan recordings. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch that content (for example a surah or verse number), never information about you. Those services may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Notifications, alarms and Do Not Disturb

Notifications (prayer alerts, reminders, garden warnings) are scheduled on the device. Focus mode can turn on Do Not Disturb only if you grant that permission, and restores it when you leave.

Children

Noor is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
